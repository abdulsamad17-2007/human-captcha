Project Description :
HUMAN CAPTCHA is a lighthearted parody of "prove you're not a robot" verification checks — instead of clicking traffic lights, you prove your humanity by answering 10 randomly picked questions (from a bank of 100, half Indian/Malayali cultural quirks, half universal human habits) about how you'd react to everyday awkward, funny, or kind moments. Your answers feed a hidden scoring system that sorts you into one of five tongue-in-cheek personas — from "Are You a Robot in Disguise?" to "100% Pure Human Heart" — presented as a shareable "Official Human Certificate" with trait meters and confetti, all wrapped in a slick, cyberpunk-styled React app that runs entirely in the browser with no backend.

The Problem (that doesn't exist):
Sometimes, instead of focusing on ourselves, we end up paying attention to others for no real reason.

The Solution (that nobody asked for):
 It's a chance to pause, reflect, and discover a little more about yourself. Stay genuine.
